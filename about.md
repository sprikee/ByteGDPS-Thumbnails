# ByteGDPS Thumbnails

Level thumbnails for **ByteGDPS**, powered by our own thumbnail server.

Thumbnails are uploaded by the ByteGDPS team via the dashboard, so what you see
is always curated for our levels. No account linking needed.

Based on [Level Thumbnails](https://github.com/Level-Thumbnails/level-thumbs-mod)
by **cdc** (idea and logo by BriftXD). Thanks to cdc, Prevter and all contributors
of the original mod.
